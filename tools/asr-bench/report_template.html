Speech model benchmark (Twitch stream audio, RTX 4060)

Speed, memory and junk

"x realtime": seconds of audio per second of compute, one 60 s chunk at a time (how the transcriber works). "GPU-h per day": for 60 h of audio a day. "Outside speech": words whose middle is more than 0.5 s away from any speech the voice-activity detector found (made-up text, or speech under loud game audio the detector missed). Leaderboard WER is on clean benchmark sets, not stream audio.

How much the models agree

No human transcript exists for this audio, so this is agreement, not accuracy: WER of the column model when the row model is taken as the reference. Low numbers between two models mean they hear the same words; the odd one out is either the best or the worst, and the listening section below tells which.

Median difference in word start times on the words both models agree on (caption timing). The model whose timing is far from all others is the suspect one.
